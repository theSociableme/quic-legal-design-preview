## Executive Positioning Principle: Why "Kitchen Table" Messaging Wins

When everyday couples, co-parents, siblings, and startup co-founders face a high-stakes agreement, they are paralyzed by two fears:
1. **The Emotional & Financial Fear of Lawyers:** Hiring two attorneys from Day 1 ($5,000–$25,000+) turns a collaborative relationship into an adversarial street fight at $500/hour.
2. **The Legal Fragility of Cheap Templates:** Filling out a $599 online quiz (like HelloPrenup) or asking ChatGPT spits out a brittle fill-in-the-blank PDF that a skilled litigator can tear apart years later by claiming *"My client was pressured into clicking 'I Agree' without real negotiation or full financial transparency."*

**The `quic.legal` Messaging Mandate:**
Never lead with *"cryptographic state machines,"* *"SHA-256 append-only ledgers,"* or *"statutory UPL avoidance."* Instead, translate every architectural advantage into **emotional safety, relationship preservation, financial common sense, and courtroom-grade peace of mind**.
